Login Fixtures

Static login pages that behave like real sites toward the browser and report what a credential filler did. Nothing typed leaves the browser.

PageShape
/login.htmlForm POST, strict (public test pair only)
/login/any.htmlForm POST, accept-any
/login/spa.htmlScript POST, form removed on success
/login/spa-keep.htmlScript POST, form kept on success
/login/two-step.htmlForm POST in two steps: username, then a password page
/login/transformed.htmlForm POST of the password's SHA-256, not the password
/login/any.html?drift=passwordAccept-any with the password field renamed (drift test)
